## Gameplay


A and D can control the character's movement left and right, the W can make the character jump, press W again in the air to double jump, and hold Shift to increase the character's movement speed.

The character can pick up fruits on the ground, and multiple fruits can be converted into life.

Characters who take damage are knocked back and remain invincible for a certain amount of time.

When your HP reaches 0, it consumes a life and is revived from the most recently activated checkpoint.
